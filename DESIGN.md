# Automotive Expert design system

## Direction
Editorial workshop luxury: a near-black service environment, warm metallic accents, and tactile photography. The page feels like a considered garage notebook rather than a generic automotive template.

## Tokens
- Ink: `#0D0D0D`
- Surface: `#121212`, secondary `#1B1B1B`
- Metallic gold: `#D4AF37`, soft gold `#C5A059`
- Paper: `#F0EEE9`, muted text `#9E9A92`
- Display: Georgia italic for human, confident emphasis
- Interface: Manrope / DM Sans stack

## Composition
The first viewport pairs a high-contrast statement with real workshop imagery and a direct health check. Sections alternate between paper, near-black, and gold to create a confident, editorial rhythm. Services use a numbered line list rather than a grid of cards.

## Interaction
The health tracker is a focused dialog. It persists the last details locally, calculates the earlier of 10,000 km or 180 days, communicates green/yellow/red status, and hands off to a prefilled WhatsApp booking conversation.
